Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 31 July 2026

Customers and authorized FEMMAS APP users may request deletion of personal information associated with FEMMAS APP, including WhatsApp conversation data, subject to legal and legitimate business-retention requirements.

How to request deletion

  1. Email femmasprint@gmail.com using the subject “FEMMAS APP Data Deletion Request.”
  2. Include the WhatsApp phone number or FEMMAS APP account email connected to the information. Do not send passwords, one-time codes or sensitive financial information.
  3. Describe whether you want specific conversation data or all information associated with your account deleted.
  4. We may ask for limited information to verify that you are the person or authorized representative connected to the request.

What happens next

FEMMAS PRINT will acknowledge the request and process verified deletion requests within 30 days, unless more time is reasonably required. We will confirm when processing is complete or explain any information that must be retained.

Information we may retain

Some records may be retained where required by Tanzanian law, accounting or tax obligations, fraud prevention, security, dispute resolution or the establishment and defence of legal claims. Retained information will remain protected and will not be used for unrelated purposes.

Disconnecting Meta or WhatsApp access

Removing an app connection from Meta or WhatsApp does not always delete records already stored in FEMMAS APP. Send the deletion request above so FEMMAS PRINT can identify and process the relevant records.

For privacy questions or deletion status, contact femmasprint@gmail.com.
